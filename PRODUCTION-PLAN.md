## Pace & projection (the plain truth)

I generate roughly **2,500–3,500 words of finished prose per working pass** (one assistant turn). At that rate:

- One 45k chapter ≈ **13–18 passes**.
- Six chapters ≈ **80–100 passes**.

This is a **multi-session project measured in weeks**, not something that finishes in one sitting. The realistic plan:

1. **Build chapter by chapter, front to back.** Finish Ch.1 to 45k before opening Ch.2, so continuity and momentum hold.
2. **Commit + push after every pass** to the `claude/tender-feynman-SqN9U` branch (already happening), so progress is never lost and you can read it on GitHub as it grows.
3. **Each pass = one or two new "scene movements"** (~2.5–3.5k), expanding from the locked skeleton with new interiority, dialogue, and Manchester/rugby/music texture — never padding (prose-craftsman rule).
4. **Revision phase after drafting.** Budget a full polish pass per chapter once it hits length — a finished book is drafting + (equal or greater) revising.
