## CHARACTERS

### Eleonora "Ella" Savage-Oluwa — heroine, POV
- **Age:** 21. Final-year university student (Manchester), creative direction / styling track.
- **Origin:** Born in **Turin, Italy**; raised in **South London (Lewisham)**. Nigerian parents.
- **Family:** Eldest child ("the strong one" since age 9). Sister **Tioluwa** (17, sharp, watches Ella). Younger brothers (referenced, unnamed). Parents below.
- **Core wound:** Has been the carrier/proof her whole life; exhausted by always being put-together; flinches at being looked after ("kindness with an invoice").
- **Wants:** To prove creative work is a viable life, not a hobby; her break.
- **Tells/voice:** Reads rooms by silhouette/palette; "looking away first is an apology"; never lets herself want things too loudly (the "cold administrative tenant").
- **Physical:** Tall. (Keep further physical description minimal/consistent — not over-specified yet.)
- **Skill:** Can "hear" a sound and build it a visual world; "scores" visuals rather than styles them.

### Callum "Cal" Vance — hero, POV
- **Age:** 22.
- **Public life:** Star **fly-half**, academy prospect, scouts watching; "the one they come to watch."
- **Secret life:** Ghost-producer **"V"** — no face, no socials, no government name on credits. ~250k streams; an acclaimed 6-track EP for a Moss Side artist; produces **Knox**.
- **Studio:** Unmarked room above a vintage shop, **Northern Quarter**, no sign, leased/paid in cash from music. Up a staircase that "smells of old paper."
- **Core wound:** Brother **Jordan** died when Cal was 16 (≈5 years / 5 years 3 months before story present). Buries Jordan's last voice-note melody (**four notes: three climb, one falls**) in every track he makes. Made himself "a ghost" — *if you're already gone, you can't be left.*
- **Tells/voice:** The "counting stillness"; reads odds/risk; "the catch can have everything else"; gives "the nod" (inherited from father); furnace of intensity under a cool lid.
- **Physical:** Tall, athletically built, broken-and-reset nose, dark hair past regulation/curling at nape. Plain black tee, dark jeans; smells of cedar.

### Jordan Vance — Cal's late older brother
- **6 years older** than Cal. Gifted bedroom producer; "heard things no one else heard."
- Died in a car crash returning from a session in **Leeds**, late, wet road, ~midnight, on a **Thursday**, when Cal was 16. Cal had left Jordan's last voice note (0:47, sent 23:14, *"listen to the bit at the end"*) unopened for two days.
- Two "graves": (1) his **room kept intact** in their mother's Catford flat (decks, milk crate of records, spider plant); (2) the **four notes buried in Cal's music.**

### Gareth Vance — Cal's father
- Ex-pro **second row**, 11 seasons, broke himself; clubs that "didn't pay enough."
- Turned grief into a **project**: making Cal the "safe son." Cold, invested, gives "the nod." Drives up from London for matches; sits 3rd row behind the posts.
- Believes music is "a waste," "no living in it" — the thing that "took" Jordan. NOT actually ashamed — his coldness is **fear** (per Cal's mother): music = the road Jordan died on.
- Key line (Ch.4/match): "Don't lose it… every week." Later (Ch.6): "Don't come to the house" (said from **fear**, not rejection).

### Cal's mother — (name TBD; keep consistent once chosen)
- **Left when Cal was 14**, unable to live inside Gareth's grief-as-project. The family's "original ghost."
- Lives in a small flat in **Catford**; keeps Jordan's room intact. Wise about the ghost-pattern; pivotal in Ch.6.

### Priya Sharma — Ella's flatmate & best friend
- Marketing student; chaos engine; immaculate; dating Tom. Ella has styled her looks. Misses nothing; "sits on the floor only for major announcements."

### Tom Bryce — rugby lock, Cal's teammate, Priya's boyfriend
- "Approximately the dimensions of a wardrobe"; laughs "like a faulty boiler." Warm, kind, loyal; never pries at Cal's secret. Connective tissue between the two worlds. Picks boots by feel ("the green ones make me brave").

### Knox (Marcus Adeyemi) — rising artist
- Rapper/alt artist; project Ella is hired to style. The **only living person who knows V = Cal** (knew him pre-fame, youth-club studio). "A weather system in a good coat." Generous, chaotic. Track "Lowlight" referenced (Ella's free reimagining got her noticed).

### Sasha Reyes — label A&R / fixer
- "Smile with her whole face and none of her eyes." Builds/seeds the "Who is V?" reveal story; the antagonist-by-ambition. Treats narrative like a producer treats elements. Later (epilogue) grudgingly respects Ella; moves to a London label.

### Imogen Clarke-Bayo — London creative-studio head
- Runs the most respected new creative studio in London. Offers Ella a **junior creative director** seat (Sept start). Straight-talking, powerful, doesn't perform power. "Most people never find out who they are because they never have to choose between what they want and who they want to be."

### Minor / referenced

- Long rhythmic sentences punctuated by short punches. British register and Manchester texture. Recurring motifs (above) used deliberately — flag if overused.
- "Said" over fancy tags. Subtext in dialogue. No info-dumping research; filter through POV.
